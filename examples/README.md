# examples/: artefacts that worked

Real artefacts whose outcome you know, kept with the reason they were good.

## Why this folder exists

Describing a standard in words does not transfer it. Showing one does. An agent asked to produce a proposal, an article or a report gets much closer to your standard from one real example than from three pages of instructions.

This is the same reason few-shot prompting works, except the examples are yours.

## What earns a place here

Only artefacts whose outcome you know. A proposal that was signed, an article that performed, a deliverable a client praised. A document you liked but never sent is not an example, it is a draft.

Strip anything confidential before it goes in, or note clearly what has been redacted.

## How to write one

One file per artefact, with the reasoning on top and the artefact below:

```markdown
---
owner: [who produced it]
verified_on: 2026-01-15
status: current
ring: 2
confidential: false
---

# [What it is, and what happened]

## Outcome
[Signed, published, praised. With the date, and a number where there is one.]

## Why it worked
[Three or four points. This is the part an agent reuses: the artefact alone
teaches shape, this teaches judgement.]

## What would not work again
[Context that has changed since. Keeps an example from ageing into a trap.]

---

[The artefact, or a link to it if it is long]
```

## Loading rule

An example loads **together with** its playbook, never instead of it. The playbook gives the method, the example gives the standard. One without the other produces either generic content in the right shape, or the right shape with no method behind it.
