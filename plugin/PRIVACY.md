# Privacy: Company Brain Template plugin

Last updated: 2026-11-18

## What the plugin does with data

The plugin is a set of instructions for Claude, written in Markdown. It contains no code, no scripts, no hooks and no MCP servers, and it makes no network calls of its own.

- **What it reads.** The files you point it at: your company brain, and the documents you choose to share during setup, such as a proposal, your terms or emails sent to clients. Those documents can contain personal data, for example the names and email addresses of your clients.
- **What it writes.** Markdown and YAML files in the folder you choose, on your machine or in your repository. Some of those files can contain personal data, for example the name of a client in a file of case studies.
- **What it sends.** Nothing. The plugin sends no data to Castaldo Solutions or to any third party. Your conversation with Claude is handled by Anthropic under the terms of your Claude plan, as it would be without the plugin.
- **What it retains.** Nothing. The plugin has no server and no storage of its own. The files it writes are yours, and you decide where they live and for how long.

## Your responsibilities

The brain you build will usually hold prices, margins and client information. Keep it in a **private** repository, and record a client's permission before naming them in anything public. The skills never write credentials, only where they are kept.
