# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a straightforward, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that keeps a project usable:

- Being direct about ideas and considerate about people
- Accepting that someone else's context is different from yours
- Giving and taking feedback on the work, not on the person
- Saying "I was wrong" when you were

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Harassment of any kind, public or private
- Personal or political attacks, insults, or derogatory comments
- Publishing someone's private information without explicit permission
- Sustained disruption of discussions

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to **info@castaldosolutions.it**. Reports are handled privately, and the reporter's identity is not disclosed.

Maintainers will remove, edit or reject contributions that do not meet these standards, and may ban a contributor temporarily or permanently for behaviour they consider inappropriate, threatening or harmful.
